To properly understand how to integrate with [[AI|agentic tools]], we need to rethink collaboration models between humans and machines and continually validate via experiments. 

Current agents use natural language as the primary interface with humans and humans have established, machine-readable processes for collaboration.

The question then is: how do we onboard our agentic colleagues? I think by

1. adapting [[AI Engagement Patterns|human collaboration models to include agents]] and by
2. enabling agents to [[Agentic Self-Improvement|self-improve and learn from their interactions with humans.]]

We need to think about how to re-use and adapt our well established human interaction models to include agents: [[AI Engagement Patterns]]
